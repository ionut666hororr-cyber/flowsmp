01 / About

MORE THAN SURVIVAL.

FLOW SMP is built for players who want Minecraft to feel dangerous again. No complicated economy, no endless grinding and no reason to hide forever. Our goal is to create a competitive environment where players interact, fight, raid and create stories together.

⚔

PvP Focused

Combat is at the heart of FLOW SMP. Prepare your gear, improve your mechanics and prove yourself against other players.

💀

Semi-Anarchy

You have much more freedom than on a traditional survival server. Build, explore, fight and raid while playing within the server's core rules.

🏴

Raid The Enemy

Bases are not guaranteed to remain hidden. Track players, discover locations and prepare your team for the perfect raid.

🌎

Seasonal Gameplay

Every season can bring new mechanics, plugins, challenges and gameplay changes that keep FLOW SMP fresh.

03 / Raiding

NO BASE IS GUARANTEED SAFE.

The smaller world border is an important part of FLOW SMP. Players are encouraged to explore, locate enemies and interact with the world. A hidden base might remain hidden for a while, but the possibility of discovery is always there.

◈
Discover Explore the world and search for signs of enemy activity.
◈
Prepare Gather equipment and coordinate your team before attacking.
◈
Attack Turn preparation into action and take the fight to your enemies.
◈
Defend Your own base can be targeted too. Build intelligently and protect what matters.

07 / Rules

FREEDOM WITH LIMITS.

FLOW SMP is Semi-Anarchy, but the server still needs a basic set of rules to keep competition playable and the community healthy.

01

No Hacking

Do not use hacked clients, cheats or unauthorized modifications that provide an unfair advantage.

02

No Exploits

Abusing serious server bugs or exploits can result in moderation action.

03

Respect Players

Competition is encouraged, but targeted harassment and unacceptable behavior are not part of the community.

04

No DDoS / Threats

Real-world attacks, threats, doxxing and attempts to harm other players outside Minecraft are prohibited.

05

Keep It Competitive

PvP, rivalries and raids are part of the experience. Keep competition inside the game.

06

Follow Staff

Staff decisions and official server announcements should be respected.
